<RailSection id="how-it-works" label="How it works" class="prose">
  <p>Each session opens with a short summary of what we have heard in one-on-one conversations with peers, then turns to open discussion. Nothing said is attributed to a person or institution.</p>
  <p>It is a peer conversation, not a sales event. There are no vendor presentations. Veritir hosts and covers the cost; afterward, attendees receive a written summary of themes.</p>
