faq:
  - q: "Is the platform available now?"
    a: "Not yet. It is in design, and we are inviting a small number of enrollment teams to join as design partners. Early access begins with the readiness assessment, run on your own data."
  - q: "What is agentic enrollment?"
    a: "Agentic enrollment is the use of AI agents that carry out enrollment work, not only answer questions. An agent is given a job, such as replying to new inquiries or following up on unfinished applications, starts on a trigger, works across the CRM and messaging tools within set limits, and hands off to a counselor when a person is needed."
  - q: "How is an AI agent different from an admissions chatbot?"
    a: "A chatbot waits for a question and answers it. An AI agent is assigned an outcome. It starts the conversation, follows up over days or weeks, updates records, and escalates to staff under rules the enrollment team sets in advance."
  - q: "Do AI agents replace admissions counselors?"
    a: "In a sound design, no. Agents take the repetitive outreach: first replies, reminders, status checks and event invitations. Counselors keep the conversations that need judgment, such as cost, aid and fit. Agents should not make or imply admission or aid decisions."
  - q: "Do we need to replace our CRM to use AI agents?"
    a: "Not to get started. The first step is to measure response times, data quality and tool overlap on the systems already in place. Consolidating or replacing tools may turn out to be a finding of that work, but it should not be a prerequisite."
  - q: "What should an enrollment team measure before an AI agent pilot?"
    a: "At minimum: median time to first response, the share of inquiries answered within a day, stage-to-stage conversion by student population, staff hours spent on repetitive outreach, and cost per enrolled student. Without that baseline a pilot cannot show what changed."

<RailSection id="problem" n="01" label="The problem">
  <h2 class="lead-sm">Enrollment management teams are squeezed four ways</h2>
  <ol class="principles">
    <li><span class="n">01</span><h3>Time.</h3><p>Inquiries arrive at all hours and counselors reach some of them. In AACRAO's October 2025 survey, 95% of institutions reported at least one admissions staffing challenge and 54% named burnout. <a href="#src-aacrao">[1]</a></p></li>
    <li><span class="n">02</span><h3>Cost.</h3><p>Each enrolled student costs more to recruit when staff turn over and tools overlap. CUPA-HR found 71% of admissions coordinators and counselors had been in the role three years or less. <a href="#src-cupa">[2]</a></p></li>
    <li><span class="n">03</span><h3>Personalization.</h3><p>First-year, transfer, adult, online and graduate prospects ask different questions on different timelines. One message plan for all of them is easy to ignore.</p></li>
    <li><span class="n">04</span><h3>Integrations.</h3><p>The CRM, student information system, application, email, texting and aid tools each hold part of the picture. Knowledge sits in too many places with too little governance. <a href="#src-ct">[3]</a></p></li>
  </ol>
</RailSection>

<RailSection id="plan" label="Step 2 · Plan">
  <h2 class="lead-sm">A pilot framework the software drafts for you</h2>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>From the assessment, the platform writes a pilot charter for your team to edit and sign. An agent cannot be switched on until these five questions have answers.</p>
  </div>
  <ol class="questions">
    <li>Which one population, and which one job, comes first?</li>
    <li>Where is the single current answer to each common question?</li>
    <li>When does a person take over, and who is it?</li>
    <li>What number, measured how, counts as success?</li>
    <li>What result would make us stop?</li>
  </ol>
</RailSection>

<RailSection id="run" label="Step 3 · Run" class="prose">
  <h2 class="lead-sm">Pre-built agents across the enrollment funnel</h2>
  <p>Each stage of the <a href="#lifecycle">enrollment flywheel</a> has an agent job that comes ready to use, with its trigger, message plan and human handoff defined. Pick the one the assessment ranked first, for one population, with a cap on how many students it reaches.</p>
</RailSection>

<RailSection id="measure" label="Step 4 · Measure" class="prose">
  <h2 class="lead-sm">Results you can defend to a cabinet</h2>
  <p>A scorecard reports each agent against the step-one baseline in plain units: replies within an hour, applications completed, hours of outreach handled. The method is shown next to every number. Those results set the starting point for the next turn of the <a href="#lifecycle">flywheel</a>.</p>
</RailSection>
