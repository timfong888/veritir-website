    <h1>We're long <em>higher ed.</em></h1>
    <p>Colleges and universities have long transformed raw talent into knowledge, livelihoods and civic life. Despite rumors of their early demise, we're betting on their next century, and we're here to help them move at the speed the moment demands.</p>
  </div>

<RailSection id="manifesto" n="01" label="Manifesto" class="manifesto">
  <p class="lead">In times of massive change, durable institutions with a long-term view matter.</p>
  <p>But a long-term view pointed at the wrong destination misses the mark.</p>
  <p>For generations, colleges and universities have been the foundation for intellectual, civic and commercial life. As society changes faster and less predictably, that same durability and long horizon can become a liability.</p>
  <p>Alternatives keep multiplying, many of them built by technology leaders in Silicon Valley who now set the pace of change. As AI reshapes knowledge work and modern research, the once-stable path from degree to career must be redrawn or lead nowhere.</p>
  <p class="turn">Veritir <em>unlocks</em> the enduring value of colleges and universities: the trust they hold, the faculty and research they gather, and the communities they convene.</p>
  <p>“If we want things to stay as they are, things will have to change.” The institutions that move while they are still strong will set the terms. We're here to help them move.</p>
</RailSection>

<RailSection id="conviction" label="Conviction" class="pull">
  <blockquote>Tech moves fast and breaks things. <span>It cannot build generations of trust.</span></blockquote>
</RailSection>

<RailSection id="principles" n="02" label="Principles">
  <ol class="principles">
    <li><span class="n">01</span><h3>Leverage what you've built.</h3><p>Trust, faculty, research, alumni and existing platforms are underused assets. Innovate on this foundation.</p></li>
    <li><span class="n">02</span><h3>Startup speed. Academic standards.</h3><p>Act on real demand from learners and employers. Launch in weeks, not semesters.</p></li>
    <li><span class="n">03</span><h3>Prove value in small steps.</h3><p>Pilot, measure, then scale. Small, reversible experiments move institutions forward without putting shared governance at risk.</p></li>
    <li><span class="n">04</span><h3>Enroll once. Learn for life.</h3><p>People will return to learn many times across a career. Universities are well placed to be where they come back.</p></li>
  </ol>
</RailSection>
